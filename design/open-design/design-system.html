Design system

One palette, every screen.

The tokens, type and parts TEF Notes is built from today, read from the source: the colours from packages/core/src/theme.ts, the type from the phone's bundled Atkinson Hyperlegible, icons from lucide. Pick a theme or an accent above; the Android, Web and Landing boards follow it.

Palette

Eleven slots per theme, Palette in theme.ts. Surfaces climb a step each: bg, card, raised.

Tints

Made from the palette by tints(): muted is fg 7%, accent brand 14%, soft 6% and subtle 16% washes, scrim black 55%.

Note colours

Nine, each a wash (bg), its ink (fg) and the mid-tone that marks it (line). Dark themes get dark washes, light themes paper.
